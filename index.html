DEN: Days, Evenings & Nights

The calendar for shift workers with a life. Your rotation, your trades, your pay and your people in one place.

DEN is a mobile app for nurses, paramedics, police, oilfield crews and anyone who works a rotation. It is in a private pilot now and comes to the App Store and Google Play in Canada soon.

Your rotation in seconds

Start from a preset like 4 on 4 off or DuPont, paint your own, or snap a photo of your roster and let DEN read it.

Trade shifts with your crew

Make a pod with the people you work with, see who is on, and send trade and cover requests.

Family can see it too

Share a private calendar link your partner can add to their own phone. No app needed, and never your pay.

Know what you earned

Track hours, overtime and premiums, and check your pay stub against the shifts you worked.

Reminders before every shift

Choose when DEN reminds you: 2 hours, 12 hours or the night before.

No ads, no data sold

DEN shows no ads and never sells your information. Delete your account from inside the app any time.
