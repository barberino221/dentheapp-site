Support

Email hello@dentheapp.com and we answer within one business day. In the app, you can also go to Settings, Help and tap Send feedback or Report a problem.

Common questions

How do I add my rotation?

Tap +, then Add rotation. Pick a preset, paint your own pattern, or import a photo, file or pasted text of your roster.

How do I share my calendar with my family?

Tap the share icon at the top of the Calendar. Send the private link; they can add it to their own phone calendar. It shows shift names and times, never pay.

How do I trade a shift?

Join or create a pod with your coworkers in the Pods tab, then long-press a day on your calendar and tap Willing to trade.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you.

Someone is being abusive in a pod.

Long-press the message to report it, or tap the person's name to block them. We review reports within 24 hours.
